  "semantic_redundancy_review": "The final attributes are physically distinct at the semantic level. Motion_intensity measures aggregate energy, cadence measures repetition rate, smoothness measures sustained rapid fluctuation, and impulsiveness measures isolated abrupt peaks. Posture_inclination measures mean global configuration, posture_variability measures changes in that global configuration, and trunk_reorientation localizes chest-observable orientation change rather than duplicating either global posture statistic. No unsupported activity identity, performance statistic or unobserved biomechanical phase is introduced."
